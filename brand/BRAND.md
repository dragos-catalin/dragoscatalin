# Dragoș Cătălin — brand book (Keystone, v1.0.0)

The source of truth for the identity of dragoscatalin.ro. Machine-readable facts live in
[`brand.json`](brand.json) and [`tokens.json`](tokens.json). Every asset is generated by a
script, so do not hand-edit an export. Concept history is in [`concepts/`](concepts/README.md).

## Platform

- **Purpose.** I build products end to end, from cloud and network to the app in your pocket.
  The identity has to say "one person, the whole stack, finished work".
- **Positioning.** For founders, Romanian SMEs, EU enterprise teams and developers. It stands
  against agency gloss and generic "AI dev" neon.
- **Personality (this, not that):** solid, not heavy · warm, not cute · precise, not cold ·
  Romanian, not folkloric · confident, not loud.
- **The mark means:** a solid D with the C carved out of it. It is one block from foundation
  to finish. The comma of **ș** is the one Romanian cue.

### Naming

- Public name: **Dragoș Cătălin**, with diacritics and without the family name. Use
  `Dragos Catalin` only where ASCII is forced (URLs, handles, keyword variants).
- The comma-below letters are **ș ț** (U+0219/U+021B), never the cedilla forms ş ţ.
- The full legal name is used only where the law or identity linking needs it: the privacy
  controller line and JSON-LD `alternateName`.

| `wordmark-light/dark.svg`   | "Dragoș Cătălin" outlined from Bricolage Grotesque 720/96/88, comma in the accent |
| `wordmark-mono-black.svg`   | One-colour wordmark                                                               |
| `lockup-light/dark.svg`     | Mark + wordmark, horizontal                                                       |
| `construction.svg`          | Keyline grid, radii, carve geometry                                               |

On the site the header and footer render the mark inline (`BrandMark`). The wordmark is live
text in Bricolage with the drawn comma (`Wordmark`). It stays selectable, sharp at any size
and readable by screen readers.

### Construction

48-unit grid. The tile has rx 11. The D runs x 8.5–39 by y 9–39, with a 15-unit bowl and 2-unit
stem corners. The C is centred at (22.5, 24), radius 6.5, stroke 5 (5.6 in the 16 px cut),
with its opening at ±40°. The optical centre of the D sits 0.75 units right of the tile centre,
which balances the open side of the C.

### Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space on every side = the C stroke width × 2 (10 grid units). At 32 px that is 7 px.
- Minimum size: mark 16 px (use `mark-16.svg`), lockup 24 px tall, wordmark alone 14 px cap
  height.
- In the header the mark is 32 px and the wordmark is 18 px (`text-lg`).

### Don'ts

- Do not outline, bevel, gradient-fill or add a glow to the mark.
- Do not rotate the mark. The C turning 90° is a hover state only.
- Do not recolour the tile. The D takes the active accent; the tile stays slate `#0f1219`.
- Do not set the name in another font, or type a cedilla ş.
- Do not put the comma anywhere but under the s.
- Do not use the mark below 16 px, or the lockup on a busy photo without the tile.

Source: OKLCH; hex fallbacks are in `tokens.json`. Neutrals are slate (hue 265). The accent is
switchable (`<html data-accent>`). The default is **Ember**.

| `fg`              | oklch(0.2 0.01 265)             | oklch(0.95 0.01 265) #ebeef5   |

| `accent` (text)   | oklch(0.5 0.19 42) #a63c00      | oklch(0.74 0.19 42) #ff824f    |
| `accent-mark` (D) | oklch(0.68 0.19 42) **#f46622** | same                           |
| `mark-tile`       | oklch(0.182 0.016 265) #0f1219  | same                           |

Switchable accents (hue / chroma): ember 42/0.19 · orange 50/0.18 · amber 75/0.16 ·
rose 15/0.19 · violet 300/0.19 · indigo 275/0.18 · cyan 215/0.14 · emerald 160/0.15.

**Proof:** `node brand/scripts/contrast-gate.mjs brand/contrast-pairs.json` passes 101 pairs
with 0 WCAG 2.2 AA failures. That covers every text token on every background in both modes,
all 8 accents as text and as fills, and the mark on the tile. APCA results are advisory.
`pnpm scan:contrast` then checks the real composited page.

## Typography

| Role    | Family              | Where                                     | Weights                    |
| ------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Display | Bricolage Grotesque | wordmark, h1, section headings, OG titles | 700–800 (wdth 88, opsz 96) |
| Body    | Geist               | everything else                           | variable                   |
| Mono    | Geist Mono          | labels, stats, code                       | variable                   |

The Bricolage subset covers Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and ȘșȚț. It is 43 KB as WOFF2,
loaded with `next/font/local` and `display: swap`, with Arial as the metric-adjusted fallback.
Generate it with `python brand/scripts/subset-fonts.py <upstream ttf>`. Check it with
`python brand/scripts/check-glyphs.py <files>`. All fonts are OFL 1.1; the licence ships
in `src/fonts/OFL.txt`.

## Motion

From `tokens.json → motion` and implemented in `src/app/globals.css § Keystone logo motion`.
It is CSS only, because the header is mounted globally and `motion/react` is banned there.

| State   | What happens                                                                                                               |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intro   | The D lands (380 ms, out-expo). The C is carved in one stroke (480 ms, +260 ms). The comma drops (320 ms spring, +700 ms). |
| Hover   | The carved C turns −90° (450 ms spring) on hover or keyboard focus of the brand link.                                      |
| Reduced | `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` shows the final frame and nothing moves.                                                  |

The intro plays **once per session**. `THEME_INIT_SCRIPT` stamps `<html data-intro>` on the
first page load only (sessionStorage `dc-intro`). The static mark equals the final frame.

Preview: [`motion/intro.webm`](motion/intro.webm) ·
[`motion/intro-frames.png`](motion/intro-frames.png) · live: [`motion/demo.html`](motion/demo.html).
